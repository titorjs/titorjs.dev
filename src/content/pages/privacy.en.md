This site collects no personal data. There are no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics or third-party trackers. Fonts are served from the site itself.

**What is stored in your browser.** If you switch the theme (light or dark), the preference is saved in your browser's local storage under the key `theme`. It only remembers your choice on this site and is never sent to a server. You can clear it from your browser settings.

**Hosting.** The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes technical connection data (such as the IP address) to deliver pages and protect the service. How Cloudflare handles it is described in the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

**Email.** If you write to [tito@titorjs.dev](mailto:tito@titorjs.dev), I use your address and message only to reply.

If any of this changes, for example by adding analytics or comments, this page will be updated first.
